How to Craft the Story?

Freytag’s Pyramid (Test Drive Arc)

Employ Freytag’s Pyramid: Craft a 10-min narrative arc building tension to climax resolution. Transform pitches into memorable stories.

  • Exposition: Origin tale + model reveal (hook with hero photo).
  • Rising Action: Expose pains—bland SUVs, badge premiums, visibility sacrifices.
  • Climax: Unveil Sierra—design/tech/pricing as problem-solver.
  • Falling Action: Showcase benefits—finance, warranty, ownership visuals.
  • Denouement: CTA—book drive, launch offers, seamless next steps.
